beamara

Will the projector fill the screen from there?

Beamara is an iPhone app for planning where a projector goes. Enter the screen and the lens's throw ratio, and it draws the room to scale: the strip of floor where the lens can stand, the two ends of the zoom, and a plain verdict such as "It fits" or "0.42 m too close", with the sum written underneath.
