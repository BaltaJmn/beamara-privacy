Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. This policy covers the Beamara app for iPhone.

what you type stays on your iPhone

The projectors, screens and rooms you enter are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Beamara has no account, no sign-in and no advertising, and it asks for no permission: it does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your photos or your contacts.

When you share a plan, Beamara makes a picture and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice.

the model list

When you open the list of projector models, at most once a day, the app downloads the current list from this site (beamara.baltajmn.dev/models.json). The request carries nothing about you or your plans; like any web request it reaches the site's host (GitHub Pages) from your IP address. The list inside the app works without a connection.

purchases

Beamara Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. To keep a record of purchases and work out totals, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-records service. It receives the App Store transaction (product, date, price and country of the storefront) together with a random identifier created by the app. That identifier is not linked to your identity and is not used to follow you across other apps or sites. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

what we do not do

No analytics of how you use the app, no advertising identifiers, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking.

removing your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. To ask about a purchase record, write to the address below with the date of the purchase.

children

Beamara is a planning tool with nothing aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
